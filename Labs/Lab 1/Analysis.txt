For my experiment, R0 = 1 occured at the beta value of 0.15. As seen in both models, the graph produced at beta = 0.15 is a 
straight line, and in a more complex model which allows for births and deaths, this would result in an endemic disease. All 
beta values above that took a parabolic arc indicative of an epidemic, with I increasing with time after the start. All beta 
values below 0.15 decreased sharply after the start, showing that people recovered faster than they were infected. This aligns
perfectly with threshold theorem.

The RK4 method performs better at course timesteps than Euler's method, with Euler's producing a strange upward spike in 
infections at the end of the time span.
